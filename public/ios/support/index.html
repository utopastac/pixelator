Email support Privacy Policy

Where is my art stored?

Drawings stay on your device. Uninstalling the app, or erasing the app’s data, removes local drawings. Export anything you want to keep before deleting the app.

How do I export?

Use the in-app export / share actions to save PNG or SVG via the system share sheet (Photos, Files, Messages, and so on).

Something looks wrong

Tell us your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the issue happened. Screenshots help. Email archgrovehouse@gmail.com.
